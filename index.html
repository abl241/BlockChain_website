What we do

Blockchain at Emory brings students from every major into blockchain through hands-on building, research, and the infrastructure we run ourselves. No prior crypto experience required.

  • Hacker Team

    Builds on-chain: hackathons, prototypes, and open-source tools like our MEV simulator.

  • Research & Investments

    Analyzes protocols and markets and publishes the findings on our Substack.

  • Education

    Workshops and sessions that take students from first wallet to first contract.

  • Governance

    Operates our Solana validator and takes part in on-chain governance.

  • Board

    Runs the club: recruiting, partnerships, and events.
