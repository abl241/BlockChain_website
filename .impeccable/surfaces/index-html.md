# Surface: Home (index.html) + Team

Scope: the home page now absorbs About, Projects, Research and the public part of Finances; Team.html restyled into the same world. Mode: Persuade. Two audiences equally: Emory students (apply) and companies/sponsors (connect). Proof: Solana validator, Bittensor report, style-factors paper, MEV simulator, real event photos, partner logos. finances.html, locked.html and the board login are out of scope until the board rules on them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The club presented as an engraved security certificate: proof of something real, issued and numbered. Refuses the crypto-club default of gradient hero, glass cards and floating stats.

OWN-WORLD: Emory navy ground (deep #0b1a38 field, #012169 panels), engraved gold ink (#f2a900 family) used only for linework, the seal and the primary action; white text. Hairline engraved rules, generated guilloché rosettes and border lattices in SVG, serial numbers and dates set in a mono face, a high-contrast engraved display serif for headings. No cards with shadows; ledger rows and framed panels.

STORY: In one viewport a visitor learns this is Emory's student blockchain org that runs real infrastructure, sees two paths (Students: Apply, Companies: Partner), then reads the ledger of work, sees events in photos, the partners, the divisions, and ends at a contact close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width certificate panel inside a gold guilloché border; a rosette seal drawn at right, logo top-left in the frame; display heading "Blockchain at Emory" large on the left, one-line claim beneath, two actions (gold Apply primary, outlined Partner with us), serial line along the bottom edge of the frame.

FORM: Security-certificate engraving, pick card (#1 of grounded list). seed:7723689fc7b7785c. Signature interaction: the guilloché linework engraves itself once on load (stroke draw), rosette slowly rotates under reduced-motion-safe rules; ledger rows reveal details on focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
